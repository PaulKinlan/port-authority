Further out

What a port can carry, and where it can end up. These lean on newer parts of the platform, so some will say no in some browsers.

Things that will bite you

  • A port you attach with addEventListener stays silent until you call port.start(). Setting onmessage starts it for you.
  • Messages posted before the other side is listening are not lost. They wait in the port. You can send a port and start talking into it straight away.
  • Nothing tells you when the other end has gone. A close event for ports has been specified, but the Chromium this was tested in does not fire it. Between pages on one origin, hold a Web Lock in the page and wait on it from the worker. Across origins, send a ping and treat silence as the answer.
  • A SharedWorker inside a cross-site iframe is partitioned by the top-level site, so a hidden hub iframe is a different hub on every site. A window of its own is first party and reaches the real one. Ports that have already been handed over are not affected.
  • A transferred port is gone from the sender. Post it twice and the second call throws.
