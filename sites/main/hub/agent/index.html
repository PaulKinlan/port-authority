Your agent

A site can ask to talk to your agent. If you say yes, it gets a port, and what it says down that port reaches one agent: the one you choose here. That can be the built-in demo agent, which has no model and only matches words to tools, or your own agent running on your machine, reached through a small bridge you start yourself.

This is separate from the sites that offer you tools. An agent you connect here can call those tools.
